Privacy & Data Policy

100% Client-Side Processing Guarantee

FrameAlch operates strictly inside your web browser using HTML5 and WebGL2. We stand by the absolute privacy of your creative works:

  • No Cloud Servers: No image, photo, camera frame, or file metadata is ever transmitted, uploaded, or saved to any remote servers.
  • Local Sandbox: Your files are processed dynamically using GPU textures in your RAM, existing solely within the browser tab's active session memory.
  • Camera Privacy: Real-time frame previews use direct local hardware device feeds and are never intercepted or logged.
  • No Tracking: FrameAlch does not employ cookies, analytics trackers, or user profiling tools.

By using FrameAlch, you retain full ownership and absolute confidentiality of all your digital media assets.
